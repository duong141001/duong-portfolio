I am a Product Builder & Agile Coordinator who turns ideas into shippable products with Game QA discipline. ✨ I care about edge cases, release stability, and cross-functional delivery cadence. 🎯

I started in Game QA, where I developed an obsessive eye for edge cases, regression gates, and release stability. Testing complex game states taught me that a feature is never finished until it survives unexpected user behaviors and real-world network friction.

Moving into Scrum Master and Producer roles, I discovered that team coordination is fundamentally about eliminating blockers early. Today, I combine that execution rigor with a Founder mindset: proactively building AI-augmented workflows, crafting technical PRDs, and ensuring what gets built delivers measurable business value.

Right now I'm building AI-powered operational workflows and behavioral systems, bridging deterministic rule engines with modern LLM prompt chains.
When I'm not shipping products, I'm analyzing software architectures, playing board games, and exploring new AI agentic patterns.
